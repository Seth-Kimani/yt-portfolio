How it works

The
Process

01 — Discovery

Deep channel diagnosis

Before making any recommendations, I study your data. Analytics, audience retention graphs, click-through rates, and content history all paint a picture that assumptions can't.

02 — Strategy

Positioning & roadmap

Clear channel positioning, content pillars, and a realistic 90-day growth plan. Strategy is only useful if it's executable — so every recommendation comes with a "how."

03 — Execution

Build & test in public

Implementation with fast feedback loops. Thumbnails, titles, and structure all get tested against real data. No guessing — we iterate based on what the algorithm and audience show us.

04 — Scale

Systematize what works

Once winning patterns emerge, we turn them into repeatable production systems — so you can grow without burning out or depending on one-off viral moments.
